# Her Hair Website

## Project Overview

## Project Overview

Her Hair is a small hair business established in 2024 in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. The business sells quality and stylish wigs and provides wig maintenance services such as washing, conditioning, styling, treatment, restoration and revamping.

The website provides customers with information about the business, its products and services, and allows customers to make enquiries or contact Her Hair.

## Website Goals

- Provide information about Her Hair.
- Showcase the wigs and wig maintenance services available.
- Display product and service prices.
- Allow customers to make product and service enquiries.
- Provide customers with contact details and business locations.

## Website Pages

- Home - Introduces Her Hair and displays featured products.
- About Us - Provides the business story, mission and vision.
- Products - Displays wigs, prices and wig maintenance services.
- Enquiry - Allows customers to enquire about products and services.
- Contact - Provides contact details, business hours, locations and a general contact form.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5 - Used to create the structure and content of the website.
- Visual Studio Code - Used to create and edit the website files.


## Project Structure

- `index.html` - Home page
- `about.html` - About Us page
- `products.html` - Products and services page
- `enquiry.html` - Product and service enquiry page
- `contact.html` - Contact page
- `images/` - Contains the website images
- `css/` - Contains CSS styling files
- `js/` - Contains JavaScript files
- `README.md` - Contains information about the project
## Features

- Navigation between all five website pages.
- Product images with descriptive alternative text.
- Product and wig maintenance service information.
- Product and service prices.
- Product and service enquiry form.
- General contact form.
- Business hours and multiple business locations.
- Semantic HTML5  elements for organised webpage structure.
