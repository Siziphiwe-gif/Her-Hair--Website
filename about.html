About Us

Our Story

Her Hair is a small hair business established in 2024 in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. The business was started with the aim of making stylish and quality wigs more accessible to customers while also providing professional wig maintenance services.

Her Hair began by selling a small selection of wigs to customers within the local community. As the business grew, its services expanded to include wig washing, conditioning, styling, treatment, restoration and revamping.

Today, Her Hair serves customers in East London and Gqeberha and aims to provide quality products, reliable services and a convenient experience for its customers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality and stylish wigs while offering reliable wig maintenance services that help customers care for and extend the lifespan of their wigs.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted and recognised hair business known for quality wigs, excellent customer service and professional wig maintenance services.
